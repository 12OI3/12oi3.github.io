## GDC Day 1

### [Balancing TCGs with Power Sorting](https://schedule.gdconf.com/session/balancing-tcgs-with-power-sorting/915558)

I have to say, the first talk is also my favorite talk among other. Nate Heiss start with an incredible opening, “I made 2000 cards in MTCG, non of then were banned.” Then, he introdcued how he balanced the cards, with the method of power sorting. To put it short, you first group the cards by their functions, then sorting them by their potential strength, thus are able to decide the parameters based on the order. When new cards are created, it will be easy to just put the card into the group, compare it to others and decide the parameters. He also believe that this can be use in whole lots of different situtaions, and I really agree. I always curious how to balance the card, and in fact, I am going to balance some skills in the current semester project in ETC. This talk was so useful and did literally opened my mind.

As I waiting for the next talk, I tried to attend the speed networking event. I was disappointed. Developers wil need to pair with each other randomly, and networking within 5 minutes. I personally think that 5 minutes are too short, and there weren't useful networking happening on me.

### [Game UX Mindset Masterclass: Principles and Methods](https://schedule.gdconf.com/session/game-ux-mindset-masterclass-principles-and-methods/914314)

Celia Hodent came here to introduced some basic of UX. Since I stated to get interest to UI/UX, I really looking forward to attend it. Still, the talk let me realize, game design and UX are something similar with two different perspectives. Some of the knowledge was already told by the Game Design course I attended this semester. For example, the Self-Determination Theory. But still, it is worth to get refresh on those knowledge.

### [The MIX + Kinda Funny](https://mediaindieexchange.com/showcases/spring-game-showcase-2026)

At the end of the day 1, I went to attend The MIX + Kinda Funny, which is a huge indie game showcase. I saw a lot of different indie games and getting a lot of idea. Here are some of the game I played. One thing that impact me most is the developers from [HYPERBEAT](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2263360/HYPERBEAT/). When I was trying thier demo, I was stun by how good the UI is. The developer encourage me to explore the art of UI, and recommand me to check out more graphic design. I really appreciate him.

## GDC Day 3

### [Mics in Spaces: The Importance of Field and Foley Recording](https://schedule.gdconf.com/session/mics-in-spaces-the-importance-of-field-and-foley-recording/915318)

Since I pitched a project about Foley recording, I believed this is a must attend for me. The first time I knew about Foley recording, is because of an interview [video](https://youtu.be/WFVLWo5B81w?si=zrbCSLTdwKwVETOI) of one of the speakers, Joanna Fang. During preparing pitching, I have reference her video several time, not to mention that one of the game I played recently, Death Stranding 2, she also particpated the sound design in the game and the sound design in the game was insane. The talk itself is really awesome, I learned a lot about the actual difference between field and Foley recording, and it makes me have a more clear idea on how are we going to execute the project in the future. After the talk overed, I have a chance to talked with Joanna, and I told her about our project. She seemed to be really interesting and willing to follow up with us in the future. This talk is the most valuable talk for me in the GDC this year.

## GDC Day 4

### ['DEATH STRANDING 2': Making of Voxel 3D UI Map](https://schedule.gdconf.com/session/death-stranding-2-making-of-voxel-3d-ui-map/914844)

Well, Hideo Kojima originally going to be here, but he didn’t. This talk was the only one related to Death Stranding 2, and I was also really curious of how they make the UI map in game. Although the talk itself is really tech art, so I could only uinderstand 50% of it, it is still so impress that they try to make every detail in the game to be perfect, althoguh I never noticed those detail when playing.

### [To Each Their Own Hell: The Journey of Writing Complex Characters in 'Limbus Company' (Presented by Project Moon)](https://schedule.gdconf.com/session/to-each-their-own-hell-the-journey-of-writing-complex-characters-in-limbus-company-presented-by-project-moon/916079)

Project Moon is a famous indie game studio in Korea, and some of the games are really famous because the plots and characters. Watching how the writteres approach to the narrartive is really cool. She talked about setting up a “hell” for characters, which included there only trauma and value. When these contradicted to each other, it can make the charcaters felt closer to players, and when the chracters face thier “hell,” it make them more memorable.

## Summary

What an amazing experience the first time attending GDC!

It was a lie.

I can sense the feeling of dread during the conference, and I didn’t feel really comfort about it. GDC kind of reflect the situation of the industry, and you can sort of notice that there are too much AI talks and too much talks that is also unrelated to how to make your game better. Plus, the lots of layoff just make everyone less engaging, and it was hard for me to network. It is hard to follow up with somebody else, since some of them won’t want to waste time on students. Still, looking at there are still many indie games showcasing during the conference, I did realized something really important. 

Act and make games, it will be important then just networking and do nothing.
