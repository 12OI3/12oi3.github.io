Building Virtual Worlds, a signature course at Carnegie Mellon University’s Entertainment Technology Center, requires us to form teams with randomly assigned teammates and build a world within two weeks based on a random prompt. Our prompt was "Fold paper in shapes," and we quickly came up with a cooperative game in which two players fold different paper planes to pop balloons. In this round, we were also required to integrate the Xbox Adaptive Controller into our project. To achieve this, we built two custom button machines that serve as the game’s interface.
