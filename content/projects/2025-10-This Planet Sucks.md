Building Virtual Worlds, a signature course at Carnegie Mellon University’s Entertainment Technology Center, requires us to form teams with randomly assigned teammates and build a world within two weeks based on a random prompt. Our prompt was "Air To Fortune," and we were given only one week to develop the project. After a brief discussion, we decided to combine an eye tracker and a flight controller to create a cooperative experience. The core concept involved two aliens whose job is to control a UFO and suck people up to make money. Another programmer and I quickly built a prototype and conducted playtests. However, the initial experience did not meet our expectations, so we introduced additional mechanics to further complicate and challenge the cooperation between the two players.
