《 》 is a visual novel with mild horror elements for mobile devices. You play as a man returning to his hometown, visiting a library to recover fragments of his past memories. The gameplay involves solving puzzles and mild horror, and you need to solve a meta puzzle involving multiple endings to reach the true ending.
