《 》 is a mildly horror visual novel for mobile devices. You play as a man returning to his hometown, visiting a library to recover fragments of his past memories. The gameplay involves solving puzzles and mild horror, while you might need to solved the meta puzzle that involved multi-ending to reach the true ending.
