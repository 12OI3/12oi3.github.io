Building Virtual Worlds, a signature course at Carnegie Mellon University’s Entertainment Technology Center, requires us to form teams with randomly assigned teammates and build a world within two weeks based on a random prompt. Our prompt was "Wave to a crowd," and we quickly came up with a VR experience where you play as the legendary singer MIKE during your last concert. In this round, we were also required to integrate Meta Quest 3 into our project, designing an indirect control system to ensure that even first-time VR users could experience our game intuitively and without confusion. To achieve this, we experimented with various mechanics and conducted extensive playtesting. Ultimately, we decided to initially limit player options to help them become familiar with the rules, and then gradually grant them more freedom to explore the world. Finally, they will experience an impactful ending.
