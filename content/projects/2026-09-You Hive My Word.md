You Hive My Word is a bee-themed incremental word game where players command hardworking “spelling bees” to generate letters, form words, and grow their production. Evolving from [Vocabutory](/project/2025-07-Vocabutory/), it combines wordplay, strategic upgrades, and playful physics to make every completed word feel rewarding. Developed solo in Unity, the project focuses on clear, satisfying interactions and progression that introduces new ways to play.
