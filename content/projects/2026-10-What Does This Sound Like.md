What Does This Sound Like? is a Foley-inspired exploration and puzzle game about listening, improvising, and rediscovering the creative possibilities hidden inside everyday sound.

## Narrative Setting

Mr. B. Foley is a senior sound designer who has spent years creating sounds for films and animation. Experienced and efficient, he has become so familiar with his craft that he rarely stops to question what things actually sound like.

While on vacation, Foley receives an emergency call from his studio: a short film has been left completely silent, and with no other sound designers available, he must finish it before the deadline.

Away from his studio and sound library, Foley has only a portable recorder, a simple editor, and whatever he can find around him. Ordinary objects become unexpected instruments as he experiments with new ways of creating sound.

What begins as an inconvenient assignment gradually becomes a journey of rediscovery. By paying attention to sounds he would normally overlook, Foley begins to recover the curiosity that first made him love sound design.

## The Journey

1. A Silent Film - An urgent assignment interrupts Foley’s vacation and gives him a film with no sound.
2. An Unfamiliar Toolbox - Without his studio, props, or sound library, Foley must improvise with a portable recorder and the world around him.
3. Curiosity Rediscovered - By listening before deciding what something should sound like, Foley begins to see—and hear—new creative possibilities.

## How It Works

1. Explore - Travel through handcrafted 2.5D environments built with the feeling of miniature sets. Observe each space and discover objects with unexpected sonic possibilities
2. Record - Interact with objects, surfaces, movements, and environments to capture the sounds they produce.
3. Edit - Arrange your recordings inside an unfinished film. Adjust their timing and volume to transform a silent scene into a complete soundscape.

## The Experience

There may be an obvious sound that a film needs—but the way you create that sound may be anything but obvious.
The experience is designed around pleasant exploration, surprising discoveries, creative puzzle-solving, and an approachable introduction to Foley and sound design.
